**Counters and weaknesses**

- **Engineer** — Gathers resources, constructs and repairs vehicles and structures. Strong vs: Nothing; keep it working. Weak vs: Any combat unit.
- **Pathfinder** — Long-sighted recon trooper. Detects cloaked units. Strong vs: Workers, cloaked infiltrators. Weak vs: Everything in a stand-up fight.
- **Ranger** — Versatile rifle infantry that can shoot ground and air. Strong vs: Light units, early air. Weak vs: Splash (Mortar, Shockframe, Colossus), Breachers.
- **Breacher** — Shotgun shock trooper with a breaching charge bonus against structures. Strong vs: Light infantry, buildings. Weak vs: Kiting ranged units, vehicles.
- **Lancer** — Rocket infantry that cracks armored targets. Strong vs: Tanks, walkers, Colossus. Weak vs: Light swarms (Skitter, Mantis), air.
- **Skyguard** — Missile trooper. Configurable: AA lock (default) or ground suppression mode. Strong vs: Aircraft. Weak vs: Melee rushes while in AA mode.
- **Field Medic** — Patches up biological allies in the field. Strong vs: Prolonged infantry fights. Weak vs: Burst damage, assassins.
- **Ghost Operative** — Permanently cloaked saboteur. EMP disrupts machine networks, towers and deals damage to mechanical units. Strong vs: Machine formations, towers. Weak vs: Detectors (scouts, towers).
- **Strider Buggy** — Fast raider with twin autoguns. Strong vs: Workers, light infantry, harassment. Weak vs: Lancers, towers, heavies.
- **Bastion Tank** — Main battle tank. Configurable: brace mode trades mobility for +range and +armor. Strong vs: Ground armies, armor. Weak vs: Lancers, Piercers, Spinecasters, bombers.
- **Mortar Carrier** — Long-range indirect fire. Outranges every tower. Strong vs: Towers, clumped infantry. Weak vs: Fast raiders, air, anything that closes in.
- **Mine Layer** — Seeds cloaked proximity mines around itself. Strong vs: Ground pushes through chokepoints. Weak vs: Air, detectors.
- **Kestrel** — Agile air-superiority fighter. Strong vs: Bombers, transports, drones. Weak vs: Ground anti-air.
- **Harrier** — Carpet bomber that levels ground formations and bases. Strong vs: Ground armies, structures. Weak vs: Interceptors, Skyguard, towers.
- **Dropship** — Carries 8 ground units and repairs nearby mechanical units. Strong vs: Flanking drops. Weak vs: Interceptors, AA.

## Global rules

- Unit cap: **200 actual units** per player (no weighted supply).
- Workers carry Iron 6 / Gold 4 / Wood 5 per trip; gather time [3.08, 4.2, 3.64] s.
